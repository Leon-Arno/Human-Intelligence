Human intelligence is the intellectual capability of the human brain to learn from experience, adapt to novel situations, understand abstract concepts, and use logic and motivation to manipulate the environment. It is a highly multifaceted trait driven by memory, emotional processing, creativity, and self-awareness

Metacognition, often described as “thinking about your thinking,” is widely considered the highest form of human intelligence. While raw IQ handles basic problem-solving, metacognition elevates this by allowing individuals to monitor, evaluate, and consciously adjust their thought processes, biases, and emotional reactions in real time.
